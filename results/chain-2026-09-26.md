# One multilingual model or a language chain? (2026-09-26)

**Question.** For a customer with English and Hindi traffic, is it better to chain models or to train one model?
The chain routes English to fast Ettin-17M and everything else to mmBERT-small.

**Setup.** MASSIVE en-US and hi-IN: the same 60 intents in both languages, all training rows (11,514 per language),
and the same 1,000 fixed test rows per language as `results/stock-vs-trained-2026-09-26.md`. The router checks
script only: a text goes to the non-English model when at least 30 % of its letters are Devanagari. Latency is the
median of 200 single-text calls on this Mac's CPU and GPU (MPS). Harness: `pipeline/chain.py`. Raw:
`data/bench/chain.jsonl`.

**Read this first.**
- **One mmBERT trained on both languages wins: 86.8 % overall** (88.6 % English, 85.0 % Hindi). It beats every chain
  (83.5–85.7 %) and every single-language model. It is also better on English than mmBERT trained on English only
  (88.6 % vs 88.0 %).
- **The Ettin chain costs 4.7 pts on English** (83.9 % vs 88.6 %) to cut English latency from 9.3 ms to 2.0 ms per
  call on CPU. At 9.3 ms the single model is already fast enough for message routing. The chain pays only when CPU
  time per message is the binding cost.
- **Ettin trained on both languages is the fast option** if one is needed: 81.4 % overall at 2.0 ms per call. That is
  5.4 pts below mmBERT, with no router to maintain.
- **mmBERT transfers across languages; Ettin does not.** mmBERT trained on English only scores 46.9 % on Hindi, and
  65.3 % on English when trained on Hindi only. Ettin scores 5.4 % and 8.9 % in the same tests.
- **The script router was near-perfect here:** 100.0 % English, 99.5 % Hindi. It is blind to romanized Hindi
  (Hinglish), which it sends to English. MASSIVE has no Hinglish, so this run cannot measure that failure.

**Answer.** The chain defeats the purpose for this data. Train one mmBERT on all languages. Use Ettin only where 2 ms
vs 9 ms on CPU matters more than about 5 pts of accuracy. The gate already chooses between them per question.

| ettin-en | 83.9 | 5.4 | 44.6 | 31.6 | 2.0 | 2.5 |
| ettin-hi | 8.9 | 77.1 | 43.0 | 50.2 | 2.0 | 2.6 |
| ettin-both | 85.0 | 77.8 | 81.4 | 101.0 | 2.0 | 2.6 |

| mmbert-hi | 65.3 | 83.4 | 74.4 | 236.2 | 9.4 | 4.5 |
| mmbert-both | 88.6 | 85.0 | 86.8 | 448.9 | 9.3 | 4.6 |
| chain: ettin-en → mmbert-both | 83.9 | 84.8 | 84.4 | – | – | – |
| chain: ettin-en → mmbert-hi | 83.9 | 83.2 | 83.5 | – | – | – |
| chain: mmbert-en → mmbert-hi | 88.0 | 83.4 | 85.7 | – | – | – |

Chains have no training time or latency of their own: they are the sum of the models they route to.
Training ran on MPS under the machine-wide GPU lock, one model at a time.
